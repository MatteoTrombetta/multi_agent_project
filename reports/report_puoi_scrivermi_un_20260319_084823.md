## Body

### Introduction
Antonio Di Pietro is a prominent figure in Italian politics, known for his role as a politician, lawyer, and former magistrate. He gained national prominence in the 1990s during the "Tangentopoli" scandal, which exposed widespread corruption in Italy. Di Pietro's career has been marked by his commitment to judicial reform and anti-corruption measures.

### Background
Born on May 2, 1950, in Montenero di Bisaccia, Di Pietro initially pursued a career in law and served as a magistrate. He became a household name in Italy as the lead prosecutor in the "Mani Pulite" (Clean Hands) investigation, which aimed to uncover corruption in the political system. Following his departure from the judiciary, he founded the Italy of Values party (Italia dei Valori), focusing on transparency and justice.

### Purpose or Problem Statement
The purpose of this report is to evaluate Di Pietro's political stance and contributions, particularly regarding the upcoming 2026 Italian referendum on the separation of careers for magistrates. This referendum seeks to address the distinct roles of judges and prosecutors within the Italian judicial system.

### Evaluation
Di Pietro has been a vocal advocate for judicial reform, emphasizing the need for a clear separation between the roles of judges and prosecutors. He argues that the current system can lead to conflicts of interest and undermine the impartiality of the judiciary. His position on the 2026 referendum reflects his longstanding commitment to ensuring that the judicial system operates independently and effectively.

### Possible Solution
To address the issues surrounding the separation of careers, Di Pietro suggests implementing a more structured framework that delineates the responsibilities of judges and prosecutors. He believes that such reforms will enhance the credibility of the judiciary and restore public trust in legal institutions.

## Conclusion
In summary, Antonio Di Pietro remains a significant figure in Italian politics, advocating for judicial reform and integrity. His perspective on the 2026 referendum regarding the separation of careers for magistrates underscores his commitment to improving the Italian judicial system. By promoting a clear distinction between the roles of judges and prosecutors, Di Pietro aims to foster a more transparent and trustworthy legal environment in Italy.
